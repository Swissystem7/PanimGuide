# Commercial boundary — PanimGuide (forever)

PanimGuide is a **textual heritage** education site about Jewish/Kabbalistic physiognomy sources. It is **not** a face-reader, diagnostic tool, or scoring product.

## Forever forbidden

- Camera / webcam capture of living faces
- Image upload of living faces
- Automated analysis, matching, or **scoring** of living faces
- Personality, talent, moral, or fate inference about a real person
- Selling character readings or "match your face" features

These refusals are non-negotiable for any commercial path (portfolio, workshop, or none).
