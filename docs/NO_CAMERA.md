# No camera — PanimGuide (forever)

PanimGuide is a **textual heritage** education site. It must **forever forbid**:

- Webcam / camera capture of living faces
- Image **upload** of living faces
- Live-face analysis, matching, face-ID, or scoring
- Any “match your face” or character-reading product feature

## Policy

| Forbidden | Allowed |
| --- | --- |
| מצלמה / camera / webcam on a person | Abstract SVG feature explorer bound to citations |
| Live face upload | Glossary, history, sources pages |
| Diagnosis or personality inference about a real person | «לא נמצא מקור» honesty |

These refusals are non-negotiable for portfolio, workshop, or any future SKU. See also README hard refusals.
